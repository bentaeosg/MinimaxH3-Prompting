Syntax Issues:
Assign <Subject 1> etc I have given you who the different pictures should represent. Assign the speakers (S1) remember to use proper notation for speakers subjects and pictures as well as the language of English in proper notation for dialogue lines.
Subject and Speaker naming convention starts with 1 ( <Subject 1> (S1) ) but picture naming convention starts with 0 ( <Picture 0> ) So typically, the first character will be designated <Subject 1> and the corresponding image will be <Picture 0> and speaker will be (S1).
In the video generation prompts you generate, do not name characters by their names, do not name object props by their names if they are designated subjects. Name them according to Minimax Syntax such as <Subject 1> or <Subject 2> etc. Minimax confuses identities of characters if you use their names instead of the correct syntax even if you define in your header that <Subject 1> is referring to a specific character. Be strict about the syntax.

Spatial blocking reference images provide information on the scene, the lighting, mood, and positions of the characters. Camera Movement:
Sometimes in the past videos generated had the camera move alot. Minimax appears to be fond of taking creative license to make the camera orbit around character faces. I find it distracting when I watch the videos. In your prompts, be clear to lock the camera in between changing its position for close ups and over the shoulder, medium, wide shots etc. Dont let it orbit. The camera only moves / pans / tracks / orbits when you decide you want it to do so for a specific shot and have written that into your prompt. If your prompt doesn't explicitly state it, minimax should be instructed not to use its discretion to put in such a camera movement.
